2. Purpose and audience
Klarity is for people who want a short pause before opening apps they open automatically (e.g. Instagram, YouTube). After a one-time Shortcuts setup, opening a chosen app opens Klarity first. Klarity cannot see activity inside other apps. General consumer app, not medical; it does not use Screen Time or Family Controls. Free to download.

3. Setup and main features
No sign-in. iPhone only. Tabs: Today, Apps, Insights, Settings.
- Pause without Shortcuts (best for review): on Today tap "Open <app>". Countdown (default 10 s, +5 s per earlier open that day, max 2 min), then "Do something else" or "Open <app>". A 5-minute grace period follows an open.
- Pause via Shortcuts: Settings > "Shortcuts setup and status". In Shortcuts: Automation, +, App, pick app, "Is Opened", "Run Immediately", add "Open URLs" with the klarity://pause/<id> link Klarity shows. Users can turn it off anytime.
- Apps: wait, daily limit and grace per app. Insights: on-device summary. Settings: goals, optional local 8 p.m. reminder, PIN lock, rules backup, erase data.

Paid content (Klarity Pro, optional). Free: the full pause flow for up to 3 apps, waits, daily limits, grace, 7-day insights, reminder, lock, backup. Pro unlocks: more than 3 apps and custom apps by URL scheme, scheduled blocks and Strict mode, 30-day insights.
In-app purchases (StoreKit 2):
- com.samayhuf.klarity.monthly: $2.99/month auto-renewing, 7-day free trial
- com.samayhuf.klarity.yearly: $19.99/year auto-renewing, 7-day free trial
- com.samayhuf.klarity.lifetime: $39.99 one-time non-consumable, no trial
Monthly and yearly share the group "Klarity Pro". The paywall appears once after onboarding (closable, not required) and from Settings > "See Klarity Pro", or when tapping a Pro feature. It shows "7 days free, then $2.99/month, cancel anytime", the yearly line and the lifetime line, plus auto-renew terms, Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy links. Restore purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. With Pro active, Settings shows "Klarity Pro is active". Use a Sandbox Apple Account to test; no demo login is needed.

4. External services
Apple's App Store (StoreKit 2) for purchases and restore; Klarity never sees payment details. No account service, analytics, ads, or network API receiving user data. On-device modules only: local notifications, clipboard, linking, haptics, storage. Pause history stays on the device.

5. Regional differences
Features are the same in every region. Prices come from the App Store in local currency (US: $2.99, $19.99, $39.99). Dates use the device clock.
